Making standardcancer therapywork better.

Elenagen is a DNA plasmid encoding p62/SQSTM1. Given alongside chemotherapy, it reshapes the tumor microenvironment, dampens chronic inflammation and strengthens the immune response against the tumor.

One plasmid. Four effects on the tumor and its surroundings.

Elenagen is supercoiled circular DNA that delivers the gene for p62/SQSTM1. Rather than competing with existing treatments, it is designed to be combined with them: chemotherapy, radiotherapy and immunotherapy.

Reduces tumor grade In spontaneous canine mammary cancers, tumor grade fell in 5 of 6 animals treated.
Changes the tumor microenvironment Raises stromal alpha-SMA and collagen 3, both linked to a better prognosis.
Dampens chronic inflammation Inhibits the systemic chronic inflammation that drives cancer initiation and progression.
Strengthens the anti-tumor immune response Counters the immune suppression in the tumor, enhancing chemo- and immunotherapies.

Peer-reviewed clinical evidence

Patients in our first trials had failed every available treatment and their disease was actively progressing. The aim was to stop that progression, and to make their tumors respond to chemotherapy again.

  • Clinical efficacy of Elenagen with gemcitabine in platinum-resistant ovarian cancer: a randomized controlled trialFrontiers in Oncology, 2024 · NCT05979298
  • Safety and efficacy of p62 DNA vaccine Elenagen in a first-in-human trial in patients with advanced solid tumorsOncotarget
  • P62-DNA-encoding plasmid reverts tumor grade, changes tumor stroma, and enhances anticancer immunityAging

Case study: chemo-resistant triple-negative breast cancer

A 48-year-old patient had been moved to hospice after her disease kept progressing through 21 courses of chemotherapy. She then received Elenagen weekly alongside CMF chemotherapy.

Partial regression of 33%
Progression

Built to scale, protected worldwide

Plasmids are simple to make and control. Elenagen is manufactured by Aldevron in Fargo, North Dakota, a specialist plasmid manufacturer, and is stable for more than three years frozen.
